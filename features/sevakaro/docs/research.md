## 2. Competitive Analysis

| Product | Charity Feature | What they do |
|---------|----------------|-------------|
| Paytm | Yes | "Donate to PM CARES" during campaigns, round-up donations |
| GPay | Yes | Google.org tie-ups, one-click donation from balance |
| CRED | Yes | "CRED Gives" — donate reward coins to causes |
| PhonePe | Yes | Bharat Pe feature — donate to charities from UPI |
| Amazon Pay | Partial | AmazonSmile (US only) — % of purchase donated |

**Key insight:** CRED's model is the closest — users donate reward/cashback currency (not real money) to causes. This is exactly the SevaKaro model.

**CRED learnings:**
- They saw high engagement on charity features because it feels "free" — users donate something they didn't earn through active effort
- Simple list UI with high-trust NGOs performed better than gamified charity
