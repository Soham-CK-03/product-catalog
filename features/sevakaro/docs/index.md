## What is SevaKaro?

SevaKaro (सेवाकरो) lets CashKaro users donate their cashback wallet balance to verified NGOs and charities. *Seva* means selfless service in Hindi — SevaKaro is CashKaro's social impact product.

---

## Feature Summary

| | |
|--|--|
| **Problem** | Users accumulate cashback they may not redeem. No way to put it toward a cause they care about. |
| **Solution** | A donation flow inside the CashKaro app: browse charities → pick one → donate from wallet. |
| **Who is it for** | CashKaro users with wallet balance who want to give back |
| **Business goal** | Increase wallet engagement, differentiate CashKaro, attract CSR partnerships |
